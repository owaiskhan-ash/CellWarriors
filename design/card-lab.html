06 · Rules

  • Every cell card shows its character. A card with no portrait is a bug.
  • Portrait crop is locked at background-position: 50% 8% on cards, 50% 6% on the board.
  • Rarity = border + glow + border width. Never foil, never stars.
  • No stats on cytokines. No number smaller than 9px. No bounce, no confetti.
  • ⚔ and ♥ are placeholders — draw real icons before the store build.
