07 · Icons — placeholders to replace before launch

⚔ ⚔ and ♥ are font glyphs, not icons. Draw replacements: a single-edged blade (amber) and a cell with a nucleus (teal). 24px grid, 2px stroke. Until then, never ship a screenshot that shows them at large size.

08 · Do not

  • No colours outside the token list. No gold foil, no gradients on text.
  • No light mode, no white backgrounds, no drop shadows softer than 14px blur.
  • No third font, no italics, no emoji anywhere in the product UI.
  • No stats on cytokines. No number smaller than 9px. No bounce or confetti.
