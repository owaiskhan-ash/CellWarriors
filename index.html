    START HERE · THE ONE EVERYONE ASKS ABOUT

    What does FURY do?

    Fury is stored damage. By itself it does nothing. Cards that consume Fury turn it into real numbers — Kaito’s Last Shift deals +8 per stack, then Fury resets to 0 (and +6 more with a guaranteed Crit if it spends your last AP). Build it with Cytokine Jab, Adrenal Brace and War Cry, then spend it into a Weakness or a Mark for the screenshot hit.

    No mechanic matches that search. Try “fury”, “stress”, “shield” or “tenacity”.
